Frequently Asked Questions
How do I book a ride?
Search for your route, browse available rides, and tap "Book" on your preferred option. Payment is deducted from your wallet automatically.
How do I offer a ride?
Go to Search, tap "Offer a Ride", fill in your route, time, seat count, and price. Your vehicle must be verified before offering rides.
Can I cancel a booked ride?
Yes, you can cancel up to 30 minutes before departure for a full refund. Late cancellations may incur a small fee.
How does the wallet work?
Your SafarShare wallet holds your balance for payments and earnings. Add money via JazzCash, EasyPaisa, or bank transfer. Withdrawals process within 24 hours.
Is SafarShare safe?
All users go through CNIC and phone verification. Every ride has live tracking, SOS emergency button, and trusted contact notifications. Vehicle documents are verified by our team.
What if my driver doesn't show up?
Report the no-show through the ride details screen within 1 hour. You'll receive a full refund plus a Rs 100 compensation credit.
